Forever Gold Tracker

Know what to sell, where to sell it and when.

A free dashboard and addon for WoW: Forever. The addon collects the data. You do not type it.

Open it in your browser:
pondzsi.github.io/wow-forever-gold-tracker

Decisions that the tool makes for you

Sell on the AH or to a vendor? The tool compares the AH price after the AH fee with the vendor price. It shows the better choice.
Craft the item or sell the materials? The tool calculates the cost of the materials and the value of the product. It shows which gives more gold.
Sell now or wait? The tool compares the latest AH price with the average of the last 7 days. It tells you when the price is high.
Can this item go on the AH? The tool finds soulbound, quest and warbound items. You do not search the AH for these items.
Which activity gives the most gold? The tool records the gold per hour of each fishing and farming session.
When do I reach my goal? The tool calculates a date from your gold income of the last 7 days. Example goal: epic mount.
What do I mail to my bank alt? The tool lists the items that each character sends to the bank alt. It also counts the mails and the postage.
Does the addon work in this game version? The addon tests the game data after each login. The tool shows which data works.

How it works

1
Play the game The addon records your gold, items and sales. Auctionator records the AH prices.
2
Type /reload WoW saves the data. The setup helper copies it to your Documents folder.
3
Open the dashboard The dashboard reads the data and shows the decisions. Your data stays on your PC.

Your bags, item by item

Item icons and in-game tooltips come from Wowhead. Border colors show the item quality.
