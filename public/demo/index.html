Forever Gold Tracker

Gold, Auction House prices and profession profit for WoW: Forever, with almost no typing.

Free, in your browser
pondzsi.github.io/wow-forever-gold-tracker

How it works

1
Play as usual The Gold Tracker addon records gold, bags, bank, skills, recipes, AH sales and fishing. Auctionator records prices.
➜
2
Type /reload or log out WoW saves the data. A small helper from setup.bat copies it where the browser can read it.
➜
3
Switch to the dashboard It syncs by itself and tells you what to sell, craft or keep. Your data stays on your PC.

What it does today

Gold & ledgerEvery gold change per character, sorted into vendor, AH, mail, repair, trainer…
Sell listSell on the AH, vendor or keep, for everything in your bags and bank, after AH fees.
AH prices & trendsAuctionator scans become a price history, with "prices are up / down" hints.
Crafting profitCraft vs. sell the materials vs. vendor. Recipes are read from the profession window.
Bag viewYour bags and bank as item slots, with Wowhead icons, tooltips and quality colors.
Fishing sessionsStart by themselves on your first catch: gold per hour and trunk contents.
Real AH feesRead from your AH sale mails, so profit math uses Forever's actual cut and deposit.
GoalsRiding, epic mount or any target, with progress and an estimated date.
Game versionsForever, Retail and Classic keep separate data. Ranked to-do list for what's left.
